================================================================================
                    GUIDY TRANSIT APP - TESTER INSTALLATION GUIDE
                              (Android & iOS Instructions)
================================================================================

Thank you for helping test the Guidy Transit App! 
Guidy is designed to help commuters navigate Greater Cairo's transit network,
including Metro, Buses (CTA, Green Bus, Mwasalat Misr), and Microbuses with 
accurate fares, real routes, and smart walking limits.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 1: ANDROID INSTALLATION INSTRUCTIONS (APK Sideload)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. DOWNLOAD THE APK:
   - Click the MEGA download link provided and download `guidy.apk` (or 
     `guidy-release.apk`) to your Android device.

2. OPEN THE FILE:
   - Once the download is complete, tap the download notification, or open 
     your phone's "Files" or "Downloads" app and tap on `guidy.apk`.

3. ALLOW UNKNOWN APPS / UNKNOWN SOURCES:
   - If prompted with "For your security, your phone is not allowed to install 
     unknown apps from this source":
     a) Tap "Settings" in the popup.
     b) Toggle ON "Allow from this source" (or "Install unknown apps").
     c) Tap the Back button to return to the installation screen.

4. GOOGLE PLAY PROTECT PROMPT:
   - Because this APK is downloaded directly (outside Google Play Store), 
     Google Play Protect may display a prompt saying:
     "Blocked by Play Protect" or "Unrecognized App".
   - Tap "More details" (or the dropdown arrow).
   - Tap "Install anyway" (تثبيت على أي حال).

5. COMPLETE INSTALLATION:
   - Tap "Install".
   - Once installed, tap "Open".

6. APP PERMISSIONS:
   - Location Permission: When prompted, select "While using the app" 
     (أثناء استخدام التطبيق). This is required so Guidy can detect your nearest 
     bus/metro station and calculate live walking directions.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 2: iOS (iPHONE) INSTALLATION INSTRUCTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

NOTE: iOS devices cannot install Android `.apk` files directly. Apple requires 
iOS apps to be distributed via TestFlight, an Ad-Hoc/Enterprise profile, or 
sideloading tools.

Below are the options available for iOS testers:

OPTION A: APPLE TESTFLIGHT (Recommended & Easiest)
--------------------------------------------------
1. Install the free "TestFlight" app from the Apple App Store on your iPhone.
2. Open the TestFlight invite link sent by the development team (or accept 
   the invitation email from TestFlight).
3. Tap "Accept", then tap "Install".
4. The app will install directly to your home screen and update automatically.

OPTION B: SIDELOADLY / ALTSTORE (Using an IPA file on Mac/PC)
-------------------------------------------------------------
If you were provided with a `guidy.ipa` file:
1. Download and install Sideloadly (https://sideloadly.io) or AltStore 
   (https://altstore.io) on your PC or Mac.
2. Connect your iPhone to your computer using a USB cable.
3. Open Sideloadly, drag and drop `guidy.ipa` into the tool.
4. Enter your Apple ID (used by Apple to sign the personal development certificate).
5. Click "Start". Once finished, Guidy will appear on your iPhone.
6. On your iPhone, go to:
   Settings -> General -> VPN & Device Management -> tap your Apple ID -> tap "Trust".
7. Open Guidy and grant Location Permission when prompted.

OPTION C: DIRECT XCODE INSTALL (For testers with a Mac)
-------------------------------------------------------
1. Clone the repository on your Mac.
2. Open `guidy-app-main/ios/Runner.xcworkspace` in Xcode.
3. Select your connected iPhone as the build target.
4. In "Signing & Capabilities", select your Apple ID/Team.
5. Press the "Play" (Build & Run) button (or Cmd + R).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 3: WHAT TO TEST & FEEDBACK CHECKLIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

When testing the app, please check:
[ ] Route Planning: Try planning trips (e.g. from your current location, or 
    between major hubs like Nasr City, Ramses, Tahrir, Hadayek Al-Ahram).
[ ] Walking Limits: Confirm that Guidy keeps walks under 1.5 km and does not 
    suggest excessive walking when transit is available.
[ ] Lines Screen: Explore the "Lines" tab and verify bus/metro/microbus routes 
    and station sequences.
[ ] Ads & Navigation Bar: Check that bottom banners and the "Start Trip" button 
    stay cleanly above your phone's navigation buttons (Home, Back, Recent Apps).
[ ] Live Navigation: Tap "Start Trip" to test turn-by-turn guidance.

Found a bug or have feedback?
Please take a screenshot and report your phone model, Android/iOS version, and a 
brief description of what happened.

Thank you for making Guidy better for everyone in Cairo!

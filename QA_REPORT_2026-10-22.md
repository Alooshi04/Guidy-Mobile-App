## 5. UX that doesn't make sense (suggestions)

1. **Place search is Egypt-wide.** "Mansheya" returned Mansheya, Egypt first, then four Mansheyas in Cairo/Delta; "Borg El Arab" put the airport first. The app already knows the rider's city (`PlacesService.rememberRiderLocation`); rank or filter by it so an Alexandria rider sees Alexandria places first.
2. **Destination names stay in the language they were searched in.** In the Arabic UI the header read "Gamal Abd Al Naser Street → Mansheya", "Borg El Arab", "Nasr City". Re-resolve the name in the UI language (a start that is the rider's own location is already localised).
3. **A "current location" street name can come back in the other script** ("المنار" in the English UI): reverse geocoding falls back to the local name. Cosmetic.
4. **Frequency vs reality.** The microbus "El-Mansheya → Abu Qir Club" line list says "Every ~45 min" while the same corridor on a route card says "Every ~3 min" and rides never wait that long. Showing the frequency for a line whose timetable is thin (and not for a corridor) would stop it looking broken.
5. **AC / double-decker buses**: a chip "Bus 20 · 25 EGP" next to "Bus 221 · 5.5 EGP" looks like a mistake. Label the bus type (the API sends it: "double-decker", "AC", "electric").
6. **Stars on the line chips** (route detail) have no legend; riders may not know they mean "recommended".
7. **"No route found"** says "Something went wrong" (now with the right icon). A coverage failure isn't something that went wrong; "We don't cover this trip yet" fits better.
8. **Simple Mode hides "Delete guest data"** and Sign out is the only account action. Play's data-deletion rules want account deletion reachable in the app; it is reachable only after turning Simple Mode off.
9. **ALL CAPS labels** in English settings/metro ("DARK MODE", "SHOW METRO ROUTE") look shouty beside the sentence-case app elsewhere. Deliberate? If not, drop the transform.
10. **Debug-only:** a Test Ad banner ("Nice job!") is shown above every screen. Expected in the debug build; the release build uses real units.
11. Header titles slide (marquee) when long: "Trip History & Savings" and a line name were cut mid-word until they scrolled. By design (see `GuidyHeader`), but easy to misread as clipping in a screenshot.
12. A Moovit-style "walk 13 min to board" is offered as the Recommended option when a bus from the start's own station exists (Sidi Gaber), see Backend 1.

## 6. Backend issues (not changed; this repo only)

Requests are `GET https://api.guidyapp.com/api/route?start_lat=..&start_lon=..&end_lat=..&end_lon=..&lang=en` (needs a normal `User-Agent`; Python's default `urllib` gets HTTP 403 from the front end). Responses are saved as `sg_man_en.json`, `vic_mon_en.json` (etc.) in the notes folder.

1. **Walk `distance_m` / minutes do not match the drawn path.** Sidi Gaber `(31.2196, 29.9422)` to `(31.2, 29.8955)`: first option says `walk_to_station` 911 m / 13 min to "Cleopatra Pedestrian Bridge" (about the straight-line distance, 882 m), but the two walking polylines in `segments` measure 36 m + 1240 m = 1276 m (street path). The live trip sheet then shows "1470 m · ~20 min". Every walking leg is likewise short by up to ~40 %, so totals and arrival times are optimistic by several minutes on long walks. Fix: use the OSRM path length/duration for `distance_m` and `walk_min`.
2. **Recommended option needs a 13-min walk to reach a microbus** from Sidi Gaber while the third option boards at "Sidi Gaber El Mahatta" (114 m). The bus with a 20 min wait is ranked behind it, which is defensible, but a rider at Sidi Gaber would just flag a microbus on the road beside the station. Worth checking that hail-anywhere boarding points are generated around the start and not only at stops far along the corridor.
3. **Victoria to Montaza** `(31.2433, 29.9736)` to `(31.2880, 30.0160)`: Recommended = minibus 4 with `walk_to_station` 1036 m (14 min) + `walk_to_destination` 900 m (12 min) + 23 min ride, 4 min wait = 48 min; the Cheapest = bus 218 with 20 min wait and a 14 min final walk. Victoria is itself a terminal for Abu Qir/Montaza lines; a ride starting within a few hundred metres, alighting nearer Montaza, is expected.
4. **Miami to Borg El Arab** `(31.268, 29.999)` to `(30.876, 29.605)`: single option, type **"Partial"**, `fare_total_egp` 31.5, `riding_min` 167, ending with `walk_to_destination` 3014 m (41 min). If a partial result must be shown, say so in the card (the app shows it as an ordinary route).
5. **`nearest_hubs` names a stop by its code.** `GET /route` from Miami to Borg El Arab airport `(30.9177, 29.6964)` returns an error body with `detail.code = "no_coverage"` with `from_destination.name = "Microbus stop 0132"` (`name_ar = "محطة ميكروباص 0132"`, 7214 m away). The app prints that in "the closest places we cover are … microbus stop 0132 near your destination". Use a real place name (or the nearest named stop/district) and consider showing the distance.
6. **Alexandria microbus line list** (`/routes`): "El-Mansheya – Abu Qir Club" has a headway of ~45 min while the routing engine treats the same corridor as ~3 min (`Every ~3 min` on the card). One of the two headway sources is wrong.
7. **Tahrir to Nasr City** `(30.0444, 31.2357)` to `(30.0626, 31.3410)`: the "Cheapest" option walks 788 m to board and 1819 m (25 min) after alighting (69 min total). Rule of thumb: when a card's walking exceeds the ride, don't offer it, or label it.
8. The routing time varies (1.4 to 5.6 s for the same kind of trip); Sidi Gaber to Mansheya took 5.2 s and `backend_compute_time_ms` 5247, vs ~2 s for Montaza/Borg El Arab. Cold cache, likely.
